📊 Amazon India – Sales & Operations Dashboard (2025)

🔍 Project Overview
            This project presents an Excel-based interactive dashboard analyzing Amazon India’s sales and operational performance for the year 2025.The dashboard is designed to support business decision‑making by tracking revenue, customer behavior, delivery performance, and regional trends.

📁 Tools Used
            Microsoft Excel
            Pivot Tables
            Slicers

Charts & KPI Cards
        

📈 Key KPIs
            Total Orders
            Total Revenue
            Total Customers
            Average Order Value (AOV)
            Total Delivered Orders
            Delivery Success Rate

📊 Dashboard Features
            Monthly Sales Trend analysis
            Revenue by Product Category
            Payment Method Distribution
            Top 5 States by Revenue
            Interactive slicers for dynamic filtering
            Key business insights section

🧠 Key Insights
            Revenue shows seasonal growth with peaks in mid‑to‑late year
            Electronics and Beauty are top revenue contributors
            Delivery success rate is low (~34%), indicating operational challenges
            Digital payments slightly outperform Cash on Delivery
            Sales are concentrated in a few high‑performing states
